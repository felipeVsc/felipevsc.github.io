abstract: "Parkinson's disease (PD) is a neurodegenerative disorder with a progressive nature. It causes motor symptoms such as resting tremor, bradykinesia, and other movement disorders. Because of its progressive nature, this disease requires continuous monitoring of motor symptoms. Health Monitoring Systems are widely used to monitor disease progression, improving treatment and minimizing drug side effects. In this research, we developed two predictive models using a supervised machine learning approach. These models can classify Parkinson's disease rest tremor between high or low frequencies, showing the intensity of Parkinson's motor symptoms. This classification allows detailed monitoring of medication effectiveness and disease progression.
