abstract: "The Superior Electoral Court (TSE), responsible for elections in Brazil, provides data on candidates and parties that have contested elections since 1933. These data constitute an important collection, serving as a basis for work in different research areas. However, the data provided by the TSE have several problems related to a lack of standardization and inconsistency, making analysis and integration with external databases difficult. This work presents CandiDATA, a standardized dataset with data from Brazilian elections. CandiDATA is available in open format and covers the electoral period between 1945 and 2020, including additional information beyond the data from the TSE."
