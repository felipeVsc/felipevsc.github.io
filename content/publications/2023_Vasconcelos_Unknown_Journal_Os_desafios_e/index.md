    abstract: "In recent years, the growth of smart cities has demanded solutions for managing massive and heterogeneous urban data with the goal of transforming raw data into information. These solutions need to handle specific characteristics of data access, format, and manipulation, requiring distinct approaches for processing, integration, and data analysis. In this work, we discuss the aspects involved in building Big Data Analytics in smart cities, highlighting the characteristics of the available solutions. To demonstrate the tools discussed in this paper, an initial experiment was conducted, building a data pipeline aimed at analyzing the bus traffic in the city of São Paulo."
