abstract: "This paper presents an end-to-end architecture for event data analysis, covering the extraction and structuring of event data from raw sources, query submission and result visualization. The goal is a turnkey solution that lets professionals from different domains obtain insights without dealing with technical complexity. We introduce a SQL-inspired declarative query system and demonstrate its applicability through a case study."
