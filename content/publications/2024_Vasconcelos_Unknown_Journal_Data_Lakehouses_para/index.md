    abstract: "Data Warehouses and Data Lakes are architectures capable of handling complex analyses; however, the growing generation of geospatial data, driven by the Internet of Things, highlights limitations in both architectures. Data Lakehouses emerge as the new state of the art for large-scale data storage, offering an integrated and low-cost solution. This paper proposes the use of Data Lakehouses for a large-scale geospatial data storage and analysis environment. In addition, a case study was implemented using municipal bus geolocation data to demonstrate the feasibility of the proposed environment."
