abstract: "In Brazil, according to data from ANTP, in cities with more than 60 thousand inhabitants, 85% of public transport trips are made using buses. Considering the issue of Urban Mobility, geospatial data produced by different devices (e.g., buses, personal cars, traffic lights, radars) offer great analytical potential, being relevant for decision-making that impacts quality of life in smart cities. In this work, we describe the process of collecting, standardizing, and enriching BRBus — a dataset with information from the geospatial monitoring of bus traffic in four Brazilian cities. BRBus is available in open format and covers data collected between 06/12/2023 and 06/20/2023."
